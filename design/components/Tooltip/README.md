Dry-wit tooltips that give a title, one wry line and the shortcut.

Add attributes with `Brolly.Tooltip.html(title, wit, keys)` or write `data-br-tip`, `data-br-wit` and `data-br-keys` yourself. They show on hover and focus and hide on Esc.

- The title is functional; the wit is optional and disappears when `Brolly.config.wit = false`.
- Tooltips never hold information that isn't available elsewhere.
