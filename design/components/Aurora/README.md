The slow-drifting violet, sky and rose light behind every Brolly page.

Put your app inside a `.br-stage`; `Brolly.init()` mounts the aurora automatically. The blobs lean gently toward the pointer.

- API: `Brolly.Aurora.mood('calm'|'focus'|'sulk')` shifts the palette and speed; `Brolly.Aurora.flare(stage, ms)` pulses it for celebrations.
- Opt out with `data-aurora="off"` (none) or `data-aurora="static"` (no pointer reaction).
- The consumer supplies the stage and the content. Colours come from `--aurora-violet`, `--aurora-sky`, `--aurora-rose`.
- Do keep one aurora per page. Don't put opaque full-width backgrounds on top of it; use glass.
