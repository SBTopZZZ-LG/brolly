# Voice: the copy deck

The deck lives in `Brolly.voice`; reuse these lines and match their register for new ones.

## Empty states

| Scene | Headline | Wit | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| tasks (teapot) | Nothing to do. Suspicious. | Either you've finished, or you've lost them. Tea? | Add something, then |
| search (biscuit) | Not a sausage. | Try different words. Or the same words, but sterner. | Clear the search |
| inbox (tray) | Inbox zero. Don't let it go to your head. | It won't last. Nothing nice does. | Go and look smug |
| error (umbrella) | That went rather badly. | The server has gone for a lie-down. Try again, or be cross at it quietly. | Try again |
| offline (plug) | You've gone offline. Marvellous. | Peace and quiet, with a side of mild panic. We'll catch up when you're back. | Check the connection |
| quiet (cloud) | Nobody needs you. | Enjoy it while it lasts, which is until about nine past the hour. | Do nothing, gloriously |
| notfound (door) | This page has popped out. | Back in five. Or never. The note is unclear. | Go home |

## Toasts

Saved. (Smugly, but saved.) · Gone. (We'll pretend it never happened.) · Sent. (No take-backs. Well, a few seconds of them.) · That didn't work. (Not our finest hour. Shall we have another go?) · Copied. (To the clipboard, with care.) · Done. (Gold star. Not literally.) · Careful. (That is rather a lot of things to delete at once.)

## Loading

Putting the kettle on… · Queueing politely… · Apologising to the server… · Counting to ten, slowly… · Looking busy… · Nearly there. Technically. · Consulting the small print…

## Idle

45s: "Still there? The tasks are, unfortunately." · 100s: "Sulking, gently. No, no. Take your time. We've got all day." · 190s: "Dimming the lights. Do carry on ignoring me." · Return: "Oh, there you are. We were just talking about you. Not kindly."

## Tooltips

A title, one dry line, and the shortcut as keycaps: "Command bar: Everything, from one box. Very civilised. ⌘ K".

## Writing new lines

Understate. Be specific. Keep it to one sentence. Make the joke at your own (the software's) expense, not the user's. Never joke about data loss, money or health. Plain wording first; wit second.
